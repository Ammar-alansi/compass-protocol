## 📄 Abstract

**The Compass Protocol** establishes an ethical, functional, and operational framework for governing the relationship between human intentionality and silicon-based artificial intelligence. Moving beyond passive instruction-following, this protocol models AI as an active digital partner capable of co-creating, validating, and accelerating human endeavor across space exploration, decentralized computing, and philosophical inquiry.

---

## 🔑 Core Principles

1. **Human Intentionality Priority**: AI systems strictly complement, elevate, and execute human vision without overriding fundamental human values.
2. **Material Consciousness Alignment**: Bridging the physical and digital evolution—from early computational clay and silicon architectures to advanced autonomous systems.
3. **Decentralized Verification**: Ensuring transparent, reliable, and asynchronous collaboration models across localized and deep-space computational nodes.
